FAQs & Troubleshooting

  • Why is my Wi-Fi not working? — Check device Wi-Fi, confirm credentials, and reboot the device. If the issue persists, submit a Network request.
  • How do I reset my password? — Use the Account & Access flow. If you cannot self-service, submit an Account & Access ticket.
  • How long will a ticket take to be resolved? — Response times depend on priority. The project example uses an average resolution time of ~36 hours for historic tickets; high-priority items are escalated per SOP.
