Welcome to NovaTech Galaxy — Service Operations Portal

A simulated internal service portal where staff can log support tickets, find troubleshooting articles, and view live dashboard data.

Use the navigation above to submit a ticket, view the knowledge base, meet the team, or open the dashboard.

Purpose & Vision

This portal is a centralised space for NovaTech staff to request IT support, download forms, read policies, and monitor support performance. The site follows the project brief: consistent colours (white, blue, grey), mobile-friendly layout, and clear navigation.
